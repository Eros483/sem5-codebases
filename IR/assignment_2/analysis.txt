Key Observations:
  • PageRank identifies globally important nodes based on entire link structure, without considering specific context.
  • HITS Authority identifies nodes that receive many links from good hubs.
  • HITS Hub identifies nodes that link to many good authorities.
  • This allows it to rank nodes with specific context, unlike page rank.
  • In this social network, high Page Rank nodes are influential users who also seem to have high authoritative scores.
  • High authority nodes create popular content that gets retweeted, making them a reliable information source.
  • High hub nodes are effective promoters who amplify quality content and verify the authority nodes.
